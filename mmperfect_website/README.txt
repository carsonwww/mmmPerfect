mmmPerfect website
==================

Files
- index.html: complete responsive one-page website. No build step or dependencies.

Preview
- Open index.html in a browser.

Publish for free with Cloudflare Pages
1. Sign in to Cloudflare and open Workers & Pages.
2. Create a Pages project and choose the direct upload option.
3. Upload index.html (or the folder containing it).
4. After deployment, open the project’s Custom domains section.
5. Add mmmperfect.com and follow Cloudflare’s DNS instructions.
6. If you also want www.mmmperfect.com, add it as a second custom domain and follow the DNS instructions.

Important
- The contact link currently uses chwe14542@colorado.edu because that is the contact email supplied for the resume. Replace it with a business email before publishing if you prefer not to display your school email publicly.
- The product illustration is a conceptual web graphic, not a photograph or a claim about a final manufactured sticker.
- The site avoids claiming measured water or energy savings. Add quantified claims only after you have reliable test data.
