How it works

Take the guesswork out of the handle.

mmmPerfect uses an intuitive color progression to make shower temperature easier to understand. The indicator is designed to sit near the shower control, right where people need it.

01

Blue means cold

A quick visual cue that the water is on the colder end of the range.

02

Green means comfortable

The green zone marks the target comfort range selected for the installation.

03

Red means hot

A clear signal that the water is moving toward the hotter end of the range.

Small change, useful reminder
